## The story

> I taught my parrot to say my name, and now it greets me every morning from its cage. It started doing that three days before I brought it home.

The second sentence turns the first and makes it worse on a second read: a charming morning greeting becomes something that knew its owner before they met. The horror is implied, never explained.
